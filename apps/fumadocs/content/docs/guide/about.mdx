The Sergipe Chess Federation (Federação Sergipana de Xadrez, FSX) was founded
on December 11, 1989, by the sports clubs Cotinguiba Esporte Clube, Associação
Atlética de Sergipe, Clube Esportivo Sergipe, and Clube dos Empregados da
Petrobras. The FSX is directly affiliated with the Brazilian Chess
Confederation (CBX) and, through it, with the International Chess Federation
(FIDE).

## Purposes

The FSX exists to:

- Administer and develop chess in the state of Sergipe in all its forms
- Govern chess at the state level and set its regulations
- Promote competitions, courses, and technical training
- Represent Sergipe chess before the CBX and its affiliates
- Register chess competitions and activities held in Sergipe
- Award chess titles, diplomas, and prizes
- Train players, coaches, instructors, and arbiters
