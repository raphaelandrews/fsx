The Sergipe Bullet Chess Championship is played online on lichess.org. The
page (`/bullet`) shows the current edition, with tournament information,
format, prizes, group standings, and playoff brackets. It is a public
[cup](/guide/cups).

## Format

The tournament has two stages:

- **Group stage**: ten groups of 4 players. Everyone plays everyone in a
  best-of-5 match. A 3–0 or 3–1 win is worth 3 points, a 3–2 win is worth 2
  points, a draw is worth 1.5 points, and a 3–2 loss is worth 1 point. The top
  two of each group go to the upper bracket; third and fourth go to the lower
  bracket.
- **Playoffs**: double elimination with an upper bracket, a lower bracket, and
  a grand final. Losing in the upper bracket drops a player to the lower
  bracket; losing in the lower bracket eliminates them. Matches are best of 5,
  except the grand final, which is best of 11.

## Prizes

The base prize is R$ 325. Entry fees collected above the base prize are added
to the final prize pool, which is split among the top five.

## Navigation

A card with the tournament image summarizes the time control, prize, type
(online), platform, and dates. The **Fase de Grupos** (group stage) section
shows each group's standings and round results. The **Playoffs** section shows
the upper and lower brackets and the grand final, with each match's score.
