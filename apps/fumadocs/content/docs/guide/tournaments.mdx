The tournament archive (`/torneios`) lists every tournament the federation has
rated, newest first, 12 per page, as a table: date, tournament (with its championship), rhythm, tier, and the
overall winner. On phones the columns collapse into the tournament cell, which then shows
date, rhythm, tier, and winner under the name. The whole row opens the tournament.

## Filters

Year, tier (Tier S, Tier A, Tier B, Escolar), rhythm (classic, rapid,
blitz), and championship combine freely. The filters live in the URL, so a
filtered list can be shared, and "Limpar filtros" clears them all.

## Tiers

A tournament's tier says how important it is, and sets the multiplier of its podiums in a player's
XP (S × 1, A × 0.7, B × 0.5, Escolar × 0.4; see [gamification](/reference/gamification)). The admin
picks it; the tournament edit page suggests one from who played.

| Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Tier S** | The Sergipano championships (Absoluto, Feminino, Rápido, Blitz, Bullet, Equipes). Set by the championship, never suggested. |
| **Tier A** | A strong field: at least 20 players, and the average rating of the 10 strongest players (before the tournament) reaches 2250 in rapid, 2040 in blitz or 2010 in classic. |
| **Tier B** | Every other rated tournament. The default. |
| **Escolar** | School events. Never suggested: not every school event is large, so the organiser's category decides. |

The strength lines are the 75th percentile of the field strengths (top-10 average) of every
non-school tournament of the same rhythm in the last 3 years with at least 20 players, rounded up to
10. Feminino is left out of the pool because its field is restricted; Sergipanos count like any
other event, and Tier A can be stronger than a Sergipano. A rhythm with fewer than 8 such events
keeps its previous line. The lines start at the values in `TIER_SUGGESTION` (rapid 2250, blitz 2040,
classic 2010).

The **Recalibrate** button on the dashboard's Tournaments page recomputes and saves them. It is a
tool, not a step: the usual flow is to add a tournament, import its rating results, read the
"Suggested tier" note and set the tier yourself. Recalibrate now and then (for example once a
season) so that suggestions for new tournaments follow the current ratings. It never changes a saved
tier, but the note on an older tournament uses whatever lines are saved when you open it. Run
`bun packages/api/scripts/tier-review.ts <local-d1.sqlite>` to list the tournaments whose tier would
change; it reads the file and writes nothing. Changing a tier changes every XP total derived from
it, so review the list before editing production.

The public pages show the tier names in English-style ("Tier S", "Tier A", "Tier B") even though
the site is in Portuguese; school events are "Escolar". The "O que é o tier?" button next to the
filters explains each tier and the XP multiplier it gives to podiums.

## Tournament page

`/torneios/$id` shows:

- two summary tiles (players rated and the biggest gain);
- the **podium**: the overall classification first, as three podium cards with the champion in the
  middle (each with the player's photo, the rating after the tournament and its variation), then
  places 4 and below as a list, then one tile per category. When teams share a place the
  classification stays a list;
- the **rating results** of every player, with the rating before, the
  variation, and the rating after, sorted by variation. Each name opens the
  [player profile](/guide/players). Tournaments without imported results say
  so instead of showing an empty table;
- a link to the Chess-Results page, when one is registered;
- the circuits the tournament is a stage of, linking to [Circuits](/guide/circuits).

The tournament history on a profile, the [champions gallery](/guide/champions),
and the circuit stage list all link to these pages.
