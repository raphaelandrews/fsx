---
title: Clubs
description: The club ranking on /clubes and each club's page.
---

The clubs page (`/clubes`) ranks clubs by **strength**: the average rating of
their five best active players, in the time control chosen at the top (classical,
rapid, or blitz). Clubs with fewer than five active players are listed without a
position, so one strong player cannot carry a club. Equal strengths share a place.

Each club also shows its **medals**: the tournament podiums (overall and
category) and finished-circuit podiums of the players who are its members today.
The data has no club history, so a player's medals move with them.

A club's page (`/clubes/<id>`) shows its strength and position in each time
control, its active and total members, its medals, and its players, active ones
first. Player profiles show their club's position next to its name.
