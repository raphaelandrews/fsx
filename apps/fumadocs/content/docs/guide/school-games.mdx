---
title: School Games
description: School standings, scoring, medals, and categories of the TV Sergipe School Games.
---

The TV Sergipe School Games (Jogos Escolares TV Sergipe) are a chess
tournament between schools. Schools are ranked by adding up their results in
the individual and team competitions.

## Categories

Each result belongs to one of 24 categories, combining:

- **Age:** Under 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, and 18 (Sub 8 to Sub 18)
- **Sex:** male and female
- **Modality:** individual and team

## Points

Points depend on the final place:

| Place | Points |
| ----- | ------ |
| 1st   | 10     |
| 2nd   | 8      |
| 3rd   | 6      |
| 4th   | 5      |
| 5th   | 4      |
| 6th   | 3      |
| 7th   | 2      |
| 8th   | 1      |

> **Note:** points go to the student or team according to their final place in
> each individual or team tournament.

## Medals

Alongside points, there is an Olympic-style medal table, ordered by gold, then
silver, then bronze. Each team result counts as **two** medals of its type; an
individual result counts as **one**. Points are not doubled for teams.

## Standings

A school's score in a category is the sum of the points it earned in that
category's individual and team competitions, male and female.

The public page **Jogos Escolares TV Sergipe** (`/tv-sergipe`) shows the school
standings. It switches between the **Medalhas** (medals) and **Pontos**
(points) views and groups them by age and scope: individual, team, totals per
sex, and overall.
