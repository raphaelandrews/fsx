This guide is for federation staff, arbiters, and players. It explains the
federation's technical norms and what each page of [fsx.org.br](https://www.fsx.org.br)
shows. Developers should start with the [architecture guide](/architecture).

The FSX keeps its own rating and title system to recognize the development of
players from Sergipe, alongside the official CBX and FIDE ratings.

## Technical norms

The public page **Normas Técnicas** (`/normas-tecnicas`) publishes these rules.

- [Titles](/guide/titles): requirements for each state title.
- [Rating variation](/guide/rating-variation): the K-factor and how rating changes are calculated.
- [School Games](/guide/school-games): school scoring, medals, and categories.

## Public site

- [Home](/guide/home): highlights, news, events, ratings, and announcements.
- [Ratings](/guide/ratings): rating tables for blitz, rapid, and classical.
- [Players](/guide/players): individual player profiles with history and ratings.
- [Titled players](/guide/titled-players): players holding FSX, CBX, or FIDE titles.
- [Champions](/guide/champions): state champions by modality and year.
- [Circuits](/guide/circuits): season circuits with stages and standings.
- [Bullet](/guide/bullet): the online Sergipe Bullet Championship.
- [Members](/guide/members): the board and arbiters.
- [Announcements](/guide/announcements): official numbered announcements.
- [About](/guide/about): history, purposes, and contacts.

## Administration

- [Admin panel](/guide/admin-panel): sections of the restricted dashboard.
- [Cups](/guide/cups): structure of bracket and group cup competitions.
