title: Circuits

---

The circuits page (`/circuitos`) shows the tournament circuits run by the FSX
and the player standings across their stages.

## Navigation

Tabs at the top switch between circuits, identified by name. The selected
circuit is kept in the URL.

## Circuit types

A circuit is shown in one of four layouts:

- **Padrão (default)**: one ranking with a column per stage (etapa).
- **Categorias (categories)**: like the default, split into category tabs.
- **Escolar (school)**: a club ranking with a per-player breakdown and a category filter.
- **Geral (overall)**: no stages; a single overall player ranking.

## Stages and podiums

Each stage is linked to the tournament where it was played. A stage lists its
podium with place, player (name or nickname), category, and points. Stages
without results show "Sem resultados" (no results).
