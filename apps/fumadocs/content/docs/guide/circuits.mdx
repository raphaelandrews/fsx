---
title: Circuits
description: FSX tournament circuits, with standings across stages.
---

The circuits page (`/circuitos`) shows the tournament circuits run by the FSX
and the player standings across their stages.

## Seasons

Each circuit is one season: a new year is a new circuit (for example
"Circuito Escolar 2027"), linked to the same championship as the earlier
seasons. Past seasons are kept, never deleted, so their champions stay on
record.

## Navigation

A season selector (shown when there is more than one season) picks the year;
tabs switch between that season's circuits. Both are kept in the URL
(`?ano=2026&circuito=…`). Under the tabs, the page says whether the season is in
progress or when it finished.

## Circuit types

A circuit is shown in one of four layouts:

- **Padrão (default)**: one ranking with a column per stage (etapa).
- **Categorias (categories)**: like the default, split into category tabs.
- **Escolar (school)**: a club ranking with a per-player breakdown and a category filter.
- **Geral (overall)**: no stages; a single overall player ranking.

## Stages and results

Each stage is linked to the tournament where it was played. A stage lists its
results with place, player (name or nickname), category, and points. Stages
without results show "Sem resultados" (no results). The standings add up each
player's points across stages.

## Final podiums

When a season ends, an admin finishes it on the circuit's edit page
(`/dashboard/circuits/<id>`). The top 3 of the standings become the season's
final podiums: one podium per category for the **Categorias** and **Escolar**
layouts (Sub 18, Sub 16, …), a single one otherwise. Players with equal points
share a place. The admin can then correct any placing, for example to apply a
tiebreak from the regulation.

A finished season shows a "Pódio final" block above its standings. To recompute
the podiums, reopen the season and finish it again. A finished season cannot be
deleted until it is reopened.
