The announcements page (`/comunicados`) lists the official announcements
(comunicados) published by the federation.

## Announcement list

Each announcement shows its number and year (for example, Comunicado 001/2026)
and a summary of its content. The list is paginated.

## Reading

Clicking an announcement opens its full text. Each announcement also has its
own address, `/comunicados/<id>`, which can be shared.
