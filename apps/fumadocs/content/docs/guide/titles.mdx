---
title: Titles
description: Requirements for the state titles awarded by the FSX.
---

The Sergipe Chess Federation awards state titles to players who meet
performance and rating criteria. "FSX rating" means the player's FSX rating in
any time control (blitz, rapid, or classical).

## GMS (Grande Mestre Sergipano, Sergipe Grandmaster)

A player earns the **GMS** title by:

1. Reaching an FSX rating of **2400**.
2. Finishing in the top 3, at least four times, in the Open (Absoluto) category of any of these championships:
   - Sergipe Blitz Championship
   - Sergipe Rapid Championship
   - Sergipe Open (Absoluto) Championship
   - Sergipe Team Championship
   - Championships approved by the FSX board as norm events
3. Winning the Sergipe Open Championship twice, **or** winning three titles in the championships listed above.

> **Note:** item 1 is mandatory, plus item 2 **or** item 3.

## MSE (Mestre Sergipano, Sergipe Master)

A player earns the **MSE** title by:

1. Reaching an FSX rating of **2300**.
2. Finishing in the top 3 at least twice in the championships listed in GMS item 2.
3. Winning the Sergipe Open Championship, **or** winning two titles in the championships listed in GMS item 2.

> **Note:** item 1 is mandatory, plus item 2 **or** item 3.

## CMS (Candidato a Mestre Sergipano, Sergipe Candidate Master)

A player earns the **CMS** title by:

1. Reaching an FSX rating of **2200**.
2. Finishing in the top 5 at least once in the Open category of the championships listed in GMS item 2.

> **Note:** **both** items are required.

## MJS (Mestre Júnior Sergipano, Sergipe Junior Master)

A player earns the **MJS** title by:

1. Reaching an FSX rating of **2100**.
2. Being **18 or younger**.

> **Note:** both items are required. The title is lost in the year the player turns 19;
> the site removes it at the first rating update of that year, from the birth date.

## MMS (Mestre Mirim Sergipano, Sergipe Youth Master)

A player earns the **MMS** title by:

1. Reaching an FSX rating of **2000**.
2. Being **14 or younger**.

> **Note:** both items are required. The title is lost in the year the player turns 15;
> the site removes it at the first rating update of that year, from the birth date.

## MFS (Mestre Feminina Sergipana, Sergipe Woman Master)

A player earns the **MFS** title by:

1. Reaching an FSX rating of **2150**.
2. Being a woman.

> **Note:** both items are required.

## MSHC (Mestre Sergipano Honoris Causa, Honorary Sergipe Master)

A player earns the **MSHC** title by having actively contributed to promoting
chess in the state of Sergipe.

> **Note:** the FSX board decides who receives this title.
