The admin panel (`/dashboard`) is the restricted area where the federation
manages the site's content. Only the configured owner account can sign in
(see [Authentication](/architecture/authentication)). The panel's interface is
in English.

## Sections

- **Dashboard**: counts of players, posts, announcements, and events.
- **Players**: create and edit players, with personal data, club, and ratings. **Rating history** on the edit page corrects or removes a tournament result and keeps the rating in sync. **Players → Titles** assigns titles.
- **Posts**: publish and edit news posts.
- **Announcements**: manage official announcements. Link the player an announcement is about, so it is listed on their profile; when none is linked, the edit page suggests players whose full name appears in the text.
- **Events**: manage chess events and their links (regulations, registration form, results).
- **Tournaments**: manage tournaments and their tier (S for Sergipano championships, A, B, or School). **Rating results** on the edit page lists the imported results and can revert all of them, for example before re-importing a corrected file.
- **Championships**: manage championships, the recurring competitions that group tournaments, cups, and circuit seasons. One-off tournaments need none.
- **Clubs**: manage chess clubs and upload their logos (SVG, PNG, WebP, or JPEG).
- **Locations**: manage cities, states, and countries and upload their flags (SVG, PNG, WebP, or JPEG).
- **Links**: link groups shown on the `/links` page.
- **Circuits**: one circuit per season, with its stages and their points. **Final podiums** finishes the season, stores its champions and runners-up per category, and lets you correct them.
- **TV Sergipe**: School Games results by school, category, and modality.
- **Tournament podiums**: record each player's place in a tournament, overall or in a category.
- **Titles**: titles the federation awards to players.
- **Roles**: board, arbiter, and teacher roles assigned to players.
- **Norms**: norms toward state titles.
- **Insignias**: insignias awarded to players.
- **Rating update** (`/rating-update`): apply tournament rating variations in bulk from an Excel sheet exported by Swiss Manager. After each import, players without a tournament in three years are marked inactive (a message says how many) and the ranking positions are recorded; a player who plays again is active automatically. Youth titles (Mestre Mirim, Mestre Júnior) whose holder reaches the title's age limit that year are removed at the same time; a warning counts youth titles that cannot expire because the holder has no birth date. **Update rankings now** does the same without an import. A player kept active by hand is marked inactive again at the next update if they still have no recent tournament.
- **Swiss Manager**: export player data for the Swiss Manager pairing software. The same export is public at `/swiss-manager`.
- **Cache**: the public cache freshness window and a purge button (or manual purge steps).
- **Backup**: instructions for exporting and restoring the D1 database.
- **User**: the signed-in user's session.

## Working in the panel

- **Lists** are paginated tables. Click a record's name to edit it; the row menu
  (⋯) also has Edit and Delete.
- **Create and edit pages** share one layout: a link back to the list, the
  record's name as the title, fields grouped in sections, and Cancel / Save at
  the bottom. Edit pages have a Delete button in the header.
- **Records inside a record** (a group's links, a circuit's stages, results, and final podiums,
  a player's titles, roles, insignias, and rating history) are tables on the
  parent's edit page, added and edited in a dialog.
- **Every delete or removal asks for confirmation** and names what will be
  deleted. A tournament with rating results, or a school with School Games
  results, cannot be deleted until those are removed; a finished circuit season
  must be reopened first.

Changes are visible in the panel immediately. Anonymous visitors may see the
previous version for up to five minutes; see
[Rendering and caching](/architecture/rendering-and-caching#freshness-after-an-edit).
