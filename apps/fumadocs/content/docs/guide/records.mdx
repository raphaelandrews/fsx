title: Records
description: The all-time records of Sergipe chess on /recordes.
---

The records page (`/recordes`) lists the top ten of each record among every
player the FSX has recorded, active or not; inactive players are marked
"inativo". Equal values share a place.

- **Maior rating** per time control (classical, rapid, blitz), with where it was
  reached, or "anterior aos registros" for a rating held before the records.
- **Mais títulos** and **Mais pódios**: overall tournament podiums.
- **Maior ganho em um torneio**: the largest single rating gain.
- **Maior sequência subindo**: the most consecutive tournaments with a rating gain.
- **Mais ativos** in the current year.
- **Maiores níveis**: the highest [player levels](/guide/players).
- **Maiores campeões por campeonato**: the three players with the most overall
  titles in each championship, with the years.

Records are computed from the same history as each player's profile, so a
record always matches the holder's profile.
