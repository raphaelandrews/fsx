---
title: Statistics and records
description: Database-wide statistics and all-time records of Sergipe chess on /estatisticas.
---

The statistics page (`/estatisticas`) starts with database-wide counts for active and registered
players, female players, tournaments, rating results, podiums, circuits, clubs, and cities with
active players. It then lists the top ten of each record among every player the FSX has recorded,
active or not; inactive players are marked "inativo". Equal values share a place. The old
`/recordes` URL redirects here.

Two charts put those numbers in context: grouped columns compare active players at current rating
thresholds across classical, rapid, and blitz, and a 100% stacked bar shows tournament shares by
competition tier (S, A, B, and School). Rating thresholds use the Absoluto ranking, which includes
female players; they do not add the Feminino subset twice.

- **Maior rating** per time control (classical, rapid, blitz), with where it was
  reached, or "anterior aos registros" for a rating held before the records.
- **Mais títulos** and **Mais pódios**: overall tournament podiums.
- **Maior ganho em um torneio**: the largest single rating gain.
- **Maior sequência subindo**: the most consecutive tournaments with a rating gain.
- **Mais ativos** in the current year.
- **Maiores níveis**: the highest [player levels](/guide/players).
- **Maiores campeões por campeonato**: the ten players with the most overall
  titles in each championship, with the years.

Records are computed from the same history as each player's profile, so a
record always matches the holder's profile.
