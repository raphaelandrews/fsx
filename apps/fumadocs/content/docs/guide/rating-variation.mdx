---
title: Rating Variation
description: The FSX K-factor rules and how rating variation is calculated.
---

The K-factor sets the maximum rating a player can gain or lose in each game.
A lower K means a smaller variation.

## K-factors

| K-factor | When it applies |
|----------|-----------------|
| **K=1**  | Tournaments where every player is 18 or younger, and the player is rated 2100 or higher. |
| **K=5**  | Tournaments where every player is 18 or younger. |
| **K=10** | The player is rated 2300 or higher. |
| **K=20** | All other cases. |

## Calculation

The variation is the difference between the actual score and the expected
score (from the Elo formula), multiplied by K.

```
Variation = K × (Score − Expected score)
```

Each tournament records the player's rating before the event and the variation
it produced. Import tournaments in the order they were played: each variation
is applied to the rating the player has when it is imported, and the history
shows results in that order. To fix a wrong variation, edit it in the player's **Rating history**
in the admin panel; the current rating and later tournaments follow
automatically. See [corrections](/architecture/domain-model#corrections).
