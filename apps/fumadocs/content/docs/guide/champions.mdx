The champions gallery (`/campeoes`) lists the winners and runners-up of each
edition of the Sergipe championships.

## Modalities

Tabs at the top switch between **Absoluto** (open), **Rápido** (rapid),
**Blitz**, **Feminino** (women), **Bullet**, and **Equipes** (teams).

## Champions table

Each modality shows a table with the tournament year, the champion, and the
runner-up. Clicking a name opens the [player profile](/guide/players). The
table is paginated, with 10 to 50 rows per page.
