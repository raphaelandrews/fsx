---
title: Ratings
description: FSX rating tables, filterable by time control, location, club, title, and age group.
---

The ratings page (`/ratings`) ranks active FSX players by their rating in one
of three time controls: classical, rapid, or blitz.

A player is **active** while they have played in the last three years: a rated
result, a podium, or a circuit stage. Each rating update marks players without
any activity in that window inactive (players registered less than three years
ago keep their place), and playing a tournament makes a player active again.

## Time controls

Tabs at the top switch between **Clássico**, **Rápido**, and **Blitz**. The
rating column shows the selected time control.

## Filters

The list can be filtered by:

- **Local**: the player's city, state, or country
- **Clubes**: the player's club
- **Títulos**: FSX or CBX/FIDE title
- **Grupo**: age group (Under 8 to Under 18, Master, Veteran, and Senior)
- **Categoria**: male or female
- **Busca**: player name

**Limpar** clears every filter. Filters are kept in the URL, so a filtered
table can be shared as a link.

## Age groups

Age groups use the player's birth year relative to the current year:

| Group   | Age in the current year |
| ------- | ----------------------- |
| Sub 8   | 8 or younger            |
| Sub 10  | 9–10                    |
| Sub 12  | 11–12                   |
| Sub 14  | 13–14                   |
| Sub 16  | 15–16                   |
| Sub 18  | 17–18                   |
| Master  | 40–50                   |
| Veterano| 51–64                   |
| Sênior  | 65 or older             |

## Table

Each row shows the position, the player (avatar and title), the rating in the
selected time control, the location (with flag), and the club (with logo). Next
to the position, ▲/▼ shows how many places the player moved in that time control
with the last rating update, and "novo" marks a player who was not ranked
before. Movement compares the overall ranking, so it is the same with or
without filters.
Clicking a name opens the [player profile](/guide/players). The table is
paginated.
