The titled-players page (`/titulados`) lists players holding an FSX title
(GMS, MSE, CMS, MFS, MSHC, MJS, or MMS) or a CBX/FIDE title (such as MN and CM).
See [Titles](/guide/titles) for the FSX requirements.

## Table

The table shows the position, the player's name, the FSX title, and the
CBX/FIDE title. Title columns can sort and filter the list, and clicking a name
opens the [player profile](/guide/players).

## Search and filters

A name search finds players quickly. Separate filters restrict the list to one
FSX or CBX/FIDE title. **Limpar** clears the filters. The table is paginated,
with 10 to 50 rows per page.
