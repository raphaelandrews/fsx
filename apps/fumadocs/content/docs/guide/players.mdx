title: Players
description: Public FSX player profiles with ratings, achievements, and tournament history.
---

Every player registered with the FSX has a public profile at
`/jogadores/<id>`, also reachable from the player search. The same profile
opens in a side panel when a player's name is clicked in the ratings,
champions, and titled-players tables.

## Profile layout

The page runs from the most to the least asked-for information. A sticky bar of
jump links, one per section shown, stays at the top while scrolling.

1. **Header**: photo, name (or nickname), club (with its
   [club ranking](/guide/clubs) position), location, and activity status. Below
   them are the title emblems, highest tier first, colored by tier (bronze,
   silver, gold, platinum), the roles, and the championships the player
   currently holds ("Atual campeão(ã)"). Then the **level**, with a bar toward
   the next one; it opens how the XP was earned. XP comes from the recorded
   history: 15 per tournament, podiums weighted by the event's tier (half for a
   category podium), circuit podiums, 30 per rating step from 2000 to 2400, and
   the player's highest title by tier. Reaching level *n* takes 25 × n × (n − 1)
   XP in total. A "Comparar com outro jogador" button closes the header.
2. **Ratings**: one tile per time control with the rating, its peak ("Pico")
   when above the current rating and, for active players, the position in the
   Absoluto ranking ("Absoluto #3") with its movement since the last rating
   update (▲/▼). Female players also see their Feminino position. The tiles are
   also the selector for the section below; the page opens on the time control
   the player has played the most.
3. **Evolução**: rating after each recent tournament and rating variation (from 3
   tournaments in the selected time control; before that a note says how many
   there are), and the ranking position after each update (once there are at
   least two).
4. **Conquistas**, **Carreira** (statistics and circuits), **Caminho para
   títulos**, **Comunicados**, **Histórico de Torneios**, and **Dados**, described
   below.

**Dados** closes the page with the full name (when a nickname is shown above)
and the FSX, CBX, and FIDE IDs; the CBX and FIDE IDs link to the official
profiles.

## Achievements

The **Conquistas** (achievements) section shows:

- **Medal totals** (gold, silver, bronze) with a one-line breakdown by tournaments,
  category podiums, finished circuit seasons, and circuit stages.
- **Badges**: rating steps of the title ladder (2000 … 2400 per time control),
  10/25/50/100 tournaments, streaks of tournaments with rating gains, firsts
  (tournament, rating gain, podium), and repeated Sergipano titles of the same kind
  ("Bicampeão(ã) · Feminino", "Tricampeão(ã)", "Pentacampeão(ã)", for each kind) and 2 or 3
  titles in consecutive years. Each badge opens a note with its date, "Conquista anterior
  aos registros da FSX" when it predates the records, or "Data desconhecida".
  Locked badges show the next step of each ladder with a progress bar ("7 de 10").
  Badges are grouped by tier (Platina, Ouro, Prata, Bronze, Próximas), and each
  note shows how rare it is ("12% dos jogadores têm").
- **More badges** (Phase 8): Veterano (5/10/20 seasons), Maratonista (6/10/15
  tournaments in a year), Tríplice (all three time controls in a year), Tríplice
  2000, Volta por cima, Grande salto (+30/+50/+80 in one tournament), Circuito
  completo, stage podiums (3/10/25), a category win per category, Década, and
  Recordista (first place in a `/estatisticas` record list).
- **Podiums** grouped by championship, with the number of titles, then other
  tournaments; category podiums name their category.

**Caminho para títulos** shows the next major FSX title goal (CMS, then MSE,
then GMS) with requirements from `/normas-tecnicas` and their progress. Eligible
youth and female-title goals appear separately. It is marked unofficial: the
board grants titles, and top-5 finishes and case-by-case championships aren't
recorded.

The season recap has a **Baixar imagem** button that draws a shareable card in
the browser.

Achievements are computed from the recorded history, so they include the
whole career and change when the history is corrected
([ADR 0006](/decisions/0006-derived-achievements)).

## Career and circuits

**Carreira** starts with the statistics: it shows tournaments played (and in how many seasons), the first
year, the best single rating gain, and the longest streak of rating gains, and
a calendar of tournaments per month for every year played. Each year opens the
player's **season recap** (`/jogadores/<id>/temporada/<ano>`): tournaments,
rating change per time control, best performance, XP gained, podiums, and the
achievements earned that year. A year without activity, or a future one, is a
404. Below it, **Circuitos** lists every circuit season the player took
part in, with stages played and points, the current position per category
while the season is in progress, and the final podiums once it is finished.

## Announcements

**Comunicados** lists the official announcements about the player (titles,
norms, decisions), newest first, linking to each one. The announcement page
links back to the player.

## History

The **Histórico de Torneios** section lists the player's tournaments, with the
rating before each event and the variation it produced.

## Comparing two players

"Comparar com outro jogador" on a profile opens `/comparar`. Pick two players; the choice stays in the URL, so
a comparison can be shared. The page shows ratings, best ratings, level,
tournaments played and medals side by side (the higher value is highlighted),
then the tournaments both played with each one's rating change. "Inverter
lados" swaps the two columns.
