---
title: Players
description: Public FSX player profiles with ratings, achievements, and tournament history.
---

Every player registered with the FSX has a public profile at
`/jogadores/<id>`, also reachable from the player search. The same profile
opens in a side panel when a player's name is clicked in the ratings,
champions, and titled-players tables.

## Profile details

The profile shows the player's name (or nickname), photo, club (with its
[club ranking](/guide/clubs) position), location, and activity status. Under the name, the player's **level** and a bar toward the
next one; it opens how the XP was earned. XP comes from the recorded history:
15 per tournament, podiums weighted by the event's tier (half for a category
podium), circuit podiums, 30 per rating step from 2000 to 2400, and the
player's highest title by tier. Reaching level *n* takes 25 × n × (n − 1) XP in total. Titles appear as emblems under the name, highest tier first,
colored by tier (bronze, silver, gold, platinum). Classical, rapid, and blitz
ratings are highlighted, each with its peak ("Pico") when above the current rating.
Compact cards below show the player's position in the Absoluto ranking among active
players ("Absoluto #3") and its movement with the last rating update (▲/▼). Female
players also see their position in the active Feminino ranking. The FSX, CBX, and
FIDE IDs link to the official profiles.

## Achievements

The **Conquistas** (achievements) section shows:

- **Medal counts** for tournaments, category podiums, finished circuit seasons,
  and circuit stages, kept apart so the many stage medals do not hide titles.
- **Badges**: rating steps of the title ladder (2000 … 2400 per time control),
  10/25/50/100 tournaments, streaks of tournaments with rating gains, firsts
  (tournament, rating gain, podium), and repeated titles ("Bicampeão(ã)",
  consecutive years). Each badge opens a note with its date, "Conquista anterior
  aos registros da FSX" when it predates the records, or "Data desconhecida".
  Locked badges show the next step of each ladder with a progress bar ("7 de 10").
  Badges are grouped by tier (Platina, Ouro, Prata, Bronze, Próximas), and each
  note shows how rare it is ("12% dos jogadores têm").
- **More badges** (Phase 8): Veterano (5/10/20 seasons), Maratonista (6/10/15
  tournaments in a year), Tríplice (all three time controls in a year), Tríplice
  2000, Volta por cima, Grande salto (+30/+50/+80 in one tournament), Circuito
  completo, stage podiums (3/10/25), a category win per category, Década, and
  Recordista (first place in a `/estatisticas` record list).
- **Podiums** grouped by championship, with the number of titles, then other
  tournaments; category podiums name their category.
- The championships the player currently holds.

**Caminho para títulos** shows the next major FSX title goal (CMS, then MSE,
then GMS) with requirements from `/normas-tecnicas` and their progress. Eligible
youth and female-title goals appear separately. It is marked unofficial: the
board grants titles, and top-5 finishes and case-by-case championships aren't
recorded.

The season recap has a **Baixar imagem** button that draws a shareable card in
the browser.

Achievements are computed from the recorded history, so they include the
whole career and change when the history is corrected
([ADR 0006](/decisions/0006-derived-achievements)).

## Statistics and circuits

**Estatísticas** shows tournaments played (and in how many seasons), the first
year, the best single rating gain, and the longest streak of rating gains, and
a calendar of tournaments per month for every year played. Each year opens the
player's **season recap** (`/jogadores/<id>/temporada/<ano>`): tournaments,
rating change per time control, best performance, XP gained, podiums, and the
achievements earned that year. A year without activity, or a future one, is a
404. **Circuitos** lists every circuit season the player took
part in, with stages played and points, the current position per category
while the season is in progress, and the final podiums once it is finished.

## Announcements

**Comunicados** lists the official announcements about the player (titles,
norms, decisions), newest first, linking to each one. The announcement page
links back to the player.

## Performance and history

The **Performance** section charts rating variation and evolution, with a
time-control selector. The **Histórico de Torneios** (tournament history)
section lists the player's tournaments, with the rating before each event and
the variation it produced.
