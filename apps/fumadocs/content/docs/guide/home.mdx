title: Home
description: What the FSX home page shows.
---

The home page (`/`) gathers the federation's latest highlights: news, events,
ratings, announcements, and frequently asked questions.

## Highlights and news

The two most recent news posts are featured at the top. The **Notícias**
(news) section below lists other recent posts and links to the full news page
(`/noticias`).

## Upcoming events

The **Próximos Eventos** (upcoming events) section lists scheduled
tournaments with date, time, type (open, closed, or school), and time control
(classical, rapid, blitz, or bullet). When available, buttons open the
registration form and the event regulations.

## Ratings

The **Rating** section shows the top FSX players, with tabs for classical,
rapid, and blitz. **Ver Rating** opens the full [ratings page](/guide/ratings).

## Novidades

Recent achievements of players (rating steps, podiums, titles, streaks),
titles awarded, and announcements about a player, newest first, from the last
60 days. Achievements count by the date of the tournament, so history entered
late never appears as news. The block is hidden while there is nothing to show,
and stays off until the feed's launch date is set.

## Announcements and FAQ

The **Comunicados** section shows the latest official announcements, each of
which opens in full. The **FAQ** answers common questions about tournament
registration, joining the FSX, and where upcoming events are published.
