The members page (`/membros`) shows the federation's board and arbiters, with
each person's photo and role.

## Board

The **Diretoria** (board) section lists the board members ordered by role:
President, Vice-President, Financial Vice-President, Technical Vice-President,
and Administrative Vice-President.

## Arbiters

The **Árbitros** (arbiters) section groups arbiters by level: National Arbiter,
Assistant Arbiter, and State Arbiter. Names are sorted alphabetically within
each group.
