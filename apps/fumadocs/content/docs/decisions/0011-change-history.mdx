---
title: "ADR 0011: Change History"
description: Admin changes to players, tournaments, and podiums are recorded after the change, kept for two years.
---

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-14

## Context

Admin edits overwrite rows. After a wrong edit or a bulk action there was no
way to see what a value used to be. The rating history already records rating
changes from tournaments and is deliberately not part of this.

## Decision

- A `change_log` table stores actor, entity (player, tournament, podium), record
  id, a label, the action, the procedure, and a JSON `{ field: [before, after] }`.
  It has no foreign keys, so a deleted record's history outlives it.
- It is written by a middleware on `adminProcedure` for an allowlist of
  mutations (`services/change-log.ts`). The middleware reads the affected rows
  before the call and after it, and stores only the fields that changed.
  Procedures are untouched; adding a mutation to the allowlist is one entry.
- The entry is written **after** the change, in its own batch, not in the
  change's batch. D1 has no interactive transactions and the allowlisted
  mutations are single statements or service calls with their own atomicity.
  A failure to record is logged as an error and never blocks or undoes the
  change.
- Retention is two years, enforced by deleting old rows in the same batch as
  every write, so no scheduled job exists.

## Consequences

A crash between the change and its entry loses that entry; the change stays.
Each tracked mutation costs two extra reads and one write. Rating results,
merges' moved history, and other entities (clubs, titles, posts) are not
covered; add them to the allowlist when they need it.
